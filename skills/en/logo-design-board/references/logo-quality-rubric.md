# Logo Quality Rubric

Use this reference before recommending finalists or critiquing a logo concept.

## Hard Gates

Reject or revise any concept that fails one of these gates:

- Not recognizable at 16 px or as a tiny social avatar.
- Fails in one color, black on white, or white on black.
- Depends on gradients, shadows, textures, tiny details, or rendering effects to make sense.
- Looks too close to a competitor, famous mark, stock icon, emoji, app icon, mascot, or protected character.
- Cannot be redrawn as clean vector geometry.
- Typography is illegible, generic, distorted by AI artifacts, or mismatched with the brand category.
- Symbol meaning creates cultural, accessibility, or category confusion.

## Scoring

Score each finalist from 1 to 5.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic fit | Generic or off-brief | Mostly aligned | Directly expresses the brand promise |
| Distinctiveness | Looks familiar or stock-like | Some ownable traits | Strongly ownable in the category |
| Simplicity | Too complex | Mostly simple | Irreducible without losing meaning |
| Memorability | Easy to forget | Recognizable after explanation | Easy to recall after one exposure |
| Scalability | Breaks at small sizes | Works with revisions | Works from favicon to signage |
| One-color strength | Needs color/effects | Acceptable in monochrome | Strong as a black shape |
| Typography | Weak or mismatched | Serviceable | Adds voice and precision |
| System potential | Single-use mark | Some extensions | Can drive a broader identity system |
| Production viability | Hard to vectorize/use | Needs cleanup | Ready for vector redrawing |
| Risk control | High collision or confusion risk | Manageable risk | Low obvious collision risk |

Total score is useful, but the hard gates outrank the score.

## Quick Tests

Run these tests before choosing a finalist:

- Squint test: blur or squint; the main silhouette still reads.
- Tiny test: inspect at 16 px, 24 px, and 32 px.
- Monochrome test: remove all color and effects.
- Reverse test: white mark on black, black mark on white.
- Memory test: describe the mark from memory in one sentence.
- Category test: place it among competitors; it is relevant but not camouflaged.
- Misread test: list unintended symbols, letters, body parts, cultural meanings, or jokes.
- Vector test: explain how to draw it with paths, circles, rectangles, strokes, and boolean operations.

## Recommendation Logic

Choose the finalist that best satisfies the brand strategy and passes the hard gates. Do not choose a concept only because it is visually flashy in a mockup.

If two concepts are close:

- Choose the simpler mark for product, app, SaaS, infrastructure, developer tools, finance, health, or enterprise contexts.
- Choose the more expressive mark for culture, education, media, events, creator brands, food, fashion, or entertainment contexts.
- Preserve a distinctive idea over decorative execution. Decoration can be changed later; the core idea must be right early.

## Final Caveat

Logo critique is not trademark clearance. Always recommend a separate trademark search and competitor collision check before production use.
